About me

Bridging research & real machines

Who I am and how I work
BK21 Fellow UIAI Lab · Ulsan

AI Researcher · Deep Learning Engineer · Signal Processing Specialist

// Prognostics & Health Management (PHM)

I'm a combined MS–PhD researcher in the Ulsan Industrial Artificial Intelligence (UIAI) Lab at the University of Ulsan, South Korea, working under Prof. Jong-Myon Kim. After earning my BS in Computer Systems Engineering from UET Peshawar (2023), I joined UIAI in March 2024 as a BK21 research fellow. My work develops interpretable, physics-aware deep learning for fault diagnosis, degradation modeling, and remaining useful life (RUL) prediction across bearings, milling machines, centrifugal pumps, and pipelines.

I work at the intersection of classical signal processing — acoustic emission, FFT/STFT/wavelets, Koopman and spectral methods — and modern machine learning, including Vision Transformers, conformal prediction, and domain adaptation. I've authored 25+ peer-reviewed papers in venues such as IEEE Access, Scientific Reports, Engineering Failure Analysis, Measurement, and MDPI Sensors, and received the Best Paper Award at FICTA 2025 (UK). I'm now seeking postdoctoral opportunities to extend trustworthy, deployable prognostics for safety-critical industrial systems.

  • Physics-informed & conformal prediction
  • Acoustic emission signal analysis
  • RUL & degradation modeling
  • Domain adaptation & transfer learning

Focus areas

Research interests

Where I spend my curiosity

Advanced ML for intelligent systems. Robust, data-efficient, generalizable deep learning for industrial and healthcare applications.

Signal processing & time-frequency intelligence. Physics-aware representation learning with FFT, STFT, wavelets and adaptive transforms for machinery health.

Fault diagnosis, prognostics & RUL. Interpretable, domain-adaptive approaches for early detection, degradation modeling and predictive maintenance.

Explainable & trustworthy AI. Transparency, reliability assessment, uncertainty quantification and human-centric explainability for safety-critical systems.

Transformers & foundation models. Vision transformers, multimodal models and generative approaches for industrial inspection and biomedical imaging.

Physics-informed & graph-based learning. Integrating domain knowledge, physics constraints and GNNs for smart sensing and complex systems.

Multimodal & low-label learning. Self-supervised, few-shot and cross-modal learning for scalable monitoring under limited annotations.

Edge AI & real-time monitoring. Lightweight models, on-device intelligence and efficient deployment for industrial IoT.

Digital twins & predictive analytics. AI-enabled digital twins for health awareness, reliability optimization and lifecycle management.
